About Us

The team behind JHA360 — Maharashtra's Google Street View Trusted Agency

Our Story

JHA360 grew out of New Bharat Skills' broader mission: helping India's businesses and youth adopt the digital tools that matter. We saw that most local businesses in Maharashtra had no real way to show customers what their space actually looked like before they walked in — so we became one of the state's few certified Google Street View Trusted Agencies to fix that.

Since then, we've photographed and published immersive 360° virtual tours for gyms, resorts, showrooms, banquet halls, and restaurants across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Pune, and Thane — each one built to Google's quality standards and published directly to Google Maps and Search.

We're a small, hands-on team. Every tour is shot, edited, and published by people who actually understand what makes a local business's Google presence work — not outsourced, not templated.

Our Mission

To give every local business in Maharashtra an honest, immersive way to be discovered online — through Google-certified 360° virtual tours that help customers walk in already knowing what to expect.

Our Vision

A future where any business, from a neighborhood gym to a rooftop restaurant, can compete for attention online with the same visual quality as national chains — starting with how they show up on Google Maps.
